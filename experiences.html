Wellness

The spa

Relax with a massage, a facial or a simple foot ritual after a day in the sun. Our therapists use coconut oil and local herbs, and the hairdresser is next door for a fresh look before dinner.

Restaurant & bar

Creole kitchen

Breakfast, lunch and dinner with the catch of the day, Creole curries and international favourites. Breakfast is $22 and half board is $78 per person, per night.

Book with meals

Pool

Between the palms

A 5 × 12 metre pool surrounded by sun loungers, with a separate shallow pool for children. Order a fresh juice from the bar and stay until the evening light.
